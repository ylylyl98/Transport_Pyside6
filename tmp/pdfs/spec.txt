III.2.d. Quick-Exchange Sample Holder 
The PCB of the quick-exchange sample holders has the following characteristics: 
• the contacts (1+, 1-), (2+, 2-), (3+, 3-), (4+, 4-) are pairwise connected to the 
pico-coax connections labelled Q1, Q2, Q3, Q4 as inner and outer contacts, 
respectively. 
• - 1+ is connected to the sample area in the middle (via SP pad) 
• - 1- is connected to the GND layer underneath the surface (also connected 
to all outside holes) 
• - 1+ and 1- can be disconnected from standard by cutting their respective 
connection south or north of sample area 
• - 1- can be connected to sample area by connecting the two pads north of 
the sample area
